Introduction

Anka Ratna ("the App") is a Vedic numerology application that helps users explore the ancient science of numbers based on their birth details. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you use Anka Ratna, you provide the following information:

  • Name — your full birth name
  • Date of Birth — day, month, year
  • Time of Birth — hour and minute (optional)

This information is used solely to calculate your numerological profile, including:

  • Moolank — Psychic Number
  • Bhagyank — Destiny Number
  • Namank — Name Number
  • Nakshatra — Birth Star
  • Kundli — Vedic Birth Chart

How We Store Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's local storage).

We do NOT:

  • Upload your data to any server
  • Store your data in any cloud database
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Transmit your data over the internet
Your personal information never leaves your device.

Data Usage

We use your information to:

  • Calculate your Vedic numerology profile
  • Display daily predictions based on your numbers
  • Show your Nakshatra and Kundli details
  • Provide personalized numerological insights

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal information with:

  • Third-party companies
  • Advertisers
  • Analytics services
  • Other users

Your data remains private and stored only on your device.

Data Security

Your information is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's security features. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device password-protected
  • Not sharing your device with untrusted individuals
  • Regularly backing up your device data

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Going to Settings → Reset Profile within the app
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

When you uninstall Anka Ratna, all locally stored data is automatically deleted.

Children's Privacy

Anka Ratna does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Third-Party Services

Currently, Anka Ratna does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

If we add any third-party services in the future (such as Google Analytics), we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we store (which is only on your device)
  • Request deletion of your data — via Settings → Reset Profile
  • Withdraw consent at any time — by uninstalling the app
